Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

At Digital Profit Advisors, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when contacting us or using our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect data when you contact us via WhatsApp, email, form, or during a demo. It may include:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and job title
  • Information about your business: industry, size, digital transformation needs
  • Website usage data (pages visited, duration, traffic source)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and schedule free digital maturity diagnostics
  • Provide personalized AI-driven business proposals
  • Improve our services and customer experience
  • Send relevant information about digital transformation (only if you give consent)

3. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. We work with secure platforms such as WhatsApp Business, GPT, CRM, and Red Hat Ansible, all with end-to-end encryption.

4. Do We Share Your Information?

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. We only use it internally to serve you and improve our services. If we work with technology partners (such as Botmaker or CERTIMIND), data sharing is done under confidentiality agreements.

5. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use cookies to track your behavior. We do not store navigation data beyond what is necessary for basic site functionality.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

You can exercise these rights by sending an email to: digital.profit.llc@gmail.com

We may update this policy at any time. Any changes will be posted here.
